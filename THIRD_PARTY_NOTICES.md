# Third-party notices

GELA depends on third-party libraries and separately downloaded speech models.
Their copyright and license terms remain their respective owners' terms and are
not replaced by GELA's project license.

Major runtime components include Python, Pillow, pystray, sounddevice,
sherpa-onnx, Vosk, Vosk language models, and the Meta Omnilingual ASR model
converted for sherpa-onnx. Model archives are intentionally excluded from this
repository; retain the license and notice files shipped with every downloaded
model and review them before redistribution.

Installers and release packages must include all notices required by the exact
dependency and model versions they distribute. This file is an overview, not a
substitute for those original licenses.
