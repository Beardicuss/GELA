# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive disagreement,
respect privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate humiliation, publication of
private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, discussions, pull requests, project
communications, and project-related public spaces. Maintainers may edit or
remove inappropriate material and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact channel on the
[Softcurse Systems website](https://softcurse-website.pages.dev/). Reports will
be reviewed as confidentially as practical. Do not file a public issue containing
personal or sensitive information.
